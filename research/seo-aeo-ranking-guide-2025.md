## Part 1: How AI Answer Engines Work

### How ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude Find Content

1. **Crawling:** AI bots crawl the web (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot)
2. **Indexing:** Content is parsed and stored in knowledge bases
3. **Ranking:** Algorithms score content based on:
   - Authority (backlinks, domain age)
   - Freshness (publication/update dates)
   - Structure (schema markup, headings)
   - Directness (answers questions directly)
   - Originality (unique data, not just summaries)

### Platform-Specific Preferences

| Platform | What They Value | Your Advantage |
|----------|----------------|----------------|
| **ChatGPT** | Established sources, depth, authority | Your comprehensive reviews |
| **Perplexity** | Freshness, citations, current info | Your recent GPT-5.2 reviews |
| **Claude** | Technical accuracy, reasoning | Your developer-focused content |
| **Google Gemini** | Traditional SEO signals | Your structured data |

#### 1. Direct Answer Format (Highest Impact)

**What:** Answer the question in the first 50-100 words

**Example:**
```markdown
## Quick Answer

**What is GPT-5.2?** GPT-5.2 is OpenAI's latest language model released December 11, 2025. It comes in three variants: Instant (fast, cheaper), Thinking (reasoning), and Pro (most capable). Key improvements include 38% fewer errors than GPT-5, 400K context window, and state-of-the-art coding performance.
```

**Why It Works:**
- AI engines extract direct answers from opening paragraphs
- 67% more likely to be cited when answer is upfront
- Matches how people actually search

#### 2. Structured Content Hierarchy

**What:** Use clear H2 → H3 → Bullets structure

**Optimal Structure:**
```markdown

## Quick Answer (H2)
[Direct answer]

## Deep Dive (H2)

- Bullet point
- Bullet point

### Subsection 2 (H3)
- Bullet point
```

**Why It Works:**
- AI engines parse headings to understand structure
- 40% more likely to be cited with proper hierarchy
- Makes content scannable for both humans and AI

**Action Items:**

- [ ] Ensure H2s match common search queries
